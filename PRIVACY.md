# Privacy Notice

English | [한국어](PRIVACY.ko.md)

TypeRogue 0.1.0 does not collect or transmit personal data.

The extension reads the names, forms, and types of Pokémon displayed in the current PokeRogue battle to calculate defensive matchups. This information remains in the current browser tab and is neither transmitted nor stored permanently.

`browser.storage.local` stores only:

- Panel position
- Panel collapsed state
- Development logging preference

You can remove these settings by deleting the extension's data in Firefox or by clicking the panel's reset button. TypeRogue contains no analytics, advertising SDK, remote tracking code, or developer-operated server.
